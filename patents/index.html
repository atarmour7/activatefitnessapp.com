Fitness based control of communication device

  • Inventor: Andrew Armour
  • Assignee: ATA IT Services, LLC
  • Filed: December 2, 2021 (App. No. 17/540,838)
  • Issued: May 30, 2023
  • Priority Date: September 7, 2016
  • Anticipated Expiration: September 7, 2037
  • Status: Active

What This Patent Covers

Systems and methods for limiting screen time available on a child device based on physical activity. A parent device specifies activity thresholds and associates them with amounts of screen time; activity data from a wearable sensor (or device-integral sensor) is converted to a screen-time value; available screen time is decremented as it is used; and access to designated functions or apps on the child device is disabled when the available screen-time value reaches zero.
